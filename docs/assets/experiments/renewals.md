# Cedar Learning — Membership Renewal Notes

This document is synthetic and independent from the support refund policy.

## Renewal schedule

Annual learning memberships renew on the anniversary of the original enrollment
date unless canceled before the renewal date. A renewal notice is sent to the
registered email address seven days before renewal.

## Canceling automatic renewal

Turning off automatic renewal prevents a future membership charge. It does not
automatically reverse a charge that already happened. The membership remains
active until the end of the paid term.

## Where to ask

Customers who want to understand a renewal charge should include the invoice
number and renewal date in their support inquiry. Do not share passwords,
full card numbers, or other payment secrets.

This note does not establish refund eligibility for workshop purchases; consult
the separate workshop refund policy for those conditions.
