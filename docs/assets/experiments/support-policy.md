# Cedar Support — Refund and Service Policy

All names, times, products, and rules in this document are fictional. This is
a synthetic dataset for RAG retrieval experiments.

## Refund eligibility

Cedar Support offers refunds on digital workshop registrations when the request
is submitted within fourteen calendar days of the purchase. Customers must
include their order number. For a purchase made more than fourteen days earlier,
the normal refund window has expired. An event canceled by Cedar Support
qualifies for a full refund regardless of the purchase date.

A refund decision is not based on whether a customer has written a negative
review or changed devices. The relevant evidence is the order date, the request
date, the order number, and whether the organizer canceled the event. Refunds
are returned to the original payment method after the request has been approved.
Card processing time depends on the customer's bank and is not promised here.

## Customer contact and escalation

Customers can request a refund through the imaginary help address listed on
their workshop receipt. Do not send payment card numbers to support agents.
The support team first checks the order number and the transaction date.
Escalation to the policy team is required when a customer claims that an event
was canceled or that the original date was recorded incorrectly.

The policy team may ask for a copy of the purchase confirmation. They do not
require a government identification document for ordinary refund requests.
When a receipt is unavailable, the team may search using the purchaser's email
address, but the outcome still depends on the eligibility rules above.

## Service availability and response expectations

The synthetic support desk operates on Mondays and Wednesdays from 09:00 to
12:00. An inquiry submitted outside those hours stays in the queue until the
next service period. The desk does not promise instant responses, and the
listed hours refer to the fictional local service schedule.

A support agent can explain the refund policy and check an existing request.
Agents cannot guarantee an exception or shorten bank processing time. If a
customer cannot access a workshop, the agent should investigate delivery
before assuming that the purchase qualifies for a refund. Technical access
issues are distinct from the normal cancellation and refund policy.

## Exceptions and sources

An exception must be approved by the policy team and should be documented
with a reason. An agent may not create new eligibility categories. For the
purposes of this synthetic experiment, customers who bought printed training
materials should consult a separate policy, which is **not provided** in this
document. An answer about printed materials should therefore acknowledge
insufficient information rather than inventing refund terms.
